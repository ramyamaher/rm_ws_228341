Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

The short version

This app keeps your travel history on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to create an account.

If you do not create an account, your data never leaves your device. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Who makes the app

Shuin is made by Ramy Maher. You can write to contact@ramymaher.com.

What the app stores

Your trips: dates, origin and destination cities and countries, means of transport, company, price, purpose, the people you travelled with, and the calculated carbon figure.

Expenses you add to a trip: date, description, amount, currency, category, and any note.

Stories and notes you write about a place.

Coordinates for cities you have visited, including any you place manually.

Your settings: display name, profile picture, home city, preferred currency, date format, and theme.

Where it is stored

On your device by default. The app uses your browser or app storage, and that copy stays on the device it was created on.

In the cloud only if you choose "By Account" in Settings. In that case your trips, stories, coordinates and expenses are stored in a database hosted by Supabase so they can sync between your own devices.

You can switch between these two modes at any time in Settings.

Photos and documents

When you import a ticket, a booking confirmation or a photo, the file is read on your device. The text and the location data are extracted locally.

The file itself is not uploaded. Only the trip details you confirm are saved.

Friends

Friends need an account: sign in with Apple or Google. Your trips can stay On Device. Only what you choose to share with a friend is sent.

For each friend you choose what they can see. Everything: countries, cities, trips and the names of the people on shared trips. Trips: countries, cities and trips. Places: countries and cities. Choose items: only what you tick, and anything new stays hidden until you tick it. New friends start at Trips. A shared trip shows its dates, cities, means of transport, trip name, number of days and carbon figure.

Never shared with friends: prices and costs, the provider (airline or operator), stories, notes, stays, photos and albums, and tickets. If you set a profile picture, a small copy of it is shared with your friends.

Everything a friend receives is encrypted on your phone for that friend only. The server stores it and cannot read it.

What the server keeps: your email address for signing in, your display name, your friend code, your friend list and requests, your "find me by email" setting, and the encrypted shares. Someone with your friend code, or your email address while "find me by email" is on, sees your display name.

Removing a friend deletes the shares between you. You can turn off "Let people find me by email" in Friends.

Deleting your account

In the app: open Settings, choose the "Data and access" tab and tap "Delete Account" at the bottom.

Without the app: write to contact@ramymaher.com from the email address of your account (the Apple or Google address you sign in with), with the subject "Delete my Shuin account". Your account is deleted within 30 days, and you get a confirmation by email.

What is deleted: your account and sign-in, your profile (display name and friend code), the encrypted cloud copy of your trips, stories, coordinates and expenses, your friend list and requests, and all shares between you and your friends on the server.

What stays: the data on your devices and any backup or export files you saved. Only you can delete those: delete the files and uninstall the app.

What is never collected

No advertising identifiers, no analytics, no crash telemetry, and no location tracking in the background.

The app does not read your contacts, your calendar or your photo library on its own. It only reads a file when you pick it.

Other services the app contacts

The app works fully offline. The map, the city list, the text reading for tickets and everything else it needs are built into the app. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is sent while you use it without signing in.

If you sign in, the app contacts Supabase to sign you in and to sync your data. Your trips, stories, coordinates and expenses are encrypted on your device before they are sent, and what you share with a friend is encrypted for that friend only. Signing in with Apple or Google goes through their sign-in page. What the server keeps is listed under Friends.

If you use Shuin in a web browser, the browser loads the app from its website the first time and then keeps it on your device, so it works offline too. Links you tap, such as a map link or a booking site, open outside the app.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and use "Delete Account" at the bottom.

If you have an online account, this permanently deletes that account and everything stored for it, including your trips, stories, expenses, profile and friend connections. This cannot be undone.

The copy on your device belongs to you and is left alone. To remove it, delete any files you exported and uninstall the app.

If you never created an account, nothing was ever stored outside your device, and the app will tell you so.

Your rights

You can export everything the app holds at any time as a CSV file, from the data menu.

You can delete everything as described above.

If you have a question about your data, contact contact@ramymaher.com.
