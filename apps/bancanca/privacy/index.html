Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Bancanca collects no data. Your accounts, transactions and statements stay on your device; we never see them.

Who we are

Bancanca is published by Ramy Maher, Switzerland. Contact: contact@ramymaher.com.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no cookies. The app works without an internet connection and sends nothing anywhere.

Where your data is kept

Your ledger is kept on your device, in the app’s own storage, which your phone’s operating system protects. Bank statements you import (CSV, PDF, camt, MT940, OFX) are read on your device and never leave it.

Backups

Every backup the app writes is encrypted (AES-256), with a key made on your device and protected by your safety word. You choose where a backup is saved, for example in Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. A copy you keep with a cloud service is held by that provider under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it. CSV and PDF reports are not encrypted, so that other programs can open them; keep them somewhere safe.

App lock

The optional PIN is never stored, only a salted hash of it. Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint are checked by your device; your face or fingerprint never reaches the app.

Not financial advice

Bancanca is an educational and organizational tool. It is not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and nothing in it is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset.

All projections and guidelines, including the assumed S&P 500 return, the property affordability and minimum-capital figures, and the "Guide Me" risk ladder, are illustrative. They use historical data and general rules of thumb. Past performance does not guarantee future results, and investments and property can lose value.

The developer is not a licensed financial adviser. Please consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions. You use Bancanca and any figures it shows at your own discretion and risk.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete All Transactions deletes your transactions in the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept on your device. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them there.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated.
