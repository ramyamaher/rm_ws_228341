Revitesse

Architecture and engineering software is built to serve 90 to 95 percent of the market. The remaining 5 to 10 percent is where the work becomes highly specific, and it is there that most of the time is lost.

Working in different offices, Ramy met the same problems again and again. Revitesse grew out of the wish to solve them for good, and to help other professionals spend less time on repetitive tasks in Revit.

The documentation project

The project began after a long time away from his home country. Ramy came to see himself as a citizen of the world: a nomad in time as much as in space, who belonged to each place only at a particular moment. For him, nostalgia is conditional, because places change faster than anyone can grasp.

James Joyce is said to have asked his aunt to visit a house in Dublin and check whether an overweight man could plausibly climb over its railings. If the city were ever burned to the ground, he wanted it to be possible to rebuild it from Ulysses.

That story is where the documentation project began: a record of places as they are now, before they become something else.

Revitesse

Revit, but faster.

Revitesse is a plugin for Autodesk Revit that takes the repetitive work out of producing drawings. It began as a paid product and is now completely free and open source, installed as a pyRevit extension.

Its tools sit in five panels on the Revit ribbon: Views, Sheets, Parameters, Revision Clouds and Annotations.

  • Create many sheets at once and place views on them in one step
  • Create shared parameters in batches, and transfer or combine their values
  • Select and filter elements, or the tags on them, by category or parameter
  • Number every door and window in the project at once
  • Number, override, list and export revision clouds
  • Find, select and replace text in annotations

Bancanca

Personal finance in a single file.

Bancanca keeps a household ledger, shows where the money goes and projects where it could go. The whole app is one HTML file: it runs offline, needs no server and no account, and the data never leaves the device. An Android app wraps the same file.

  • A ledger for several people, accounts and currencies
  • Bank statement import and reconciliation
  • Spending analysis, budgets and recurring payments
  • Investment and property projections, adjusted for inflation
  • Summary and tax reports
  • PIN lock and dark mode

Shuin 朱印

A world travel stamp.

A shuin is the vermilion seal a Japanese temple gives its visitors. Shuin, the app, turns every trip into a map: routes are drawn across the world, each country visited earns a stamp in a passport of 200 designs, and the whole journey can be played back as an animation. It runs in the browser and on Android.

  • An interactive world map of routes and cities
  • A passport with a stamp for every country
  • Journey playback along a timeline
  • Statistics, expenses and the CO2 of every leg
  • Shareable images of the map
  • Friends and shared trips
