The wall, unrolled

A spiral stair with a landing after three steps. The view on the way up mattered most: the stair had to open up and show more of its surroundings as you climb, not shut you in between walls. Its outer wall follows the stair up, and the line of its top edge decides how open the stair feels and how smoothly the rail runs: parallel keeps the wall closed, a sine opens it but meets the parapet with a kink, and the smooth curve opens it and flows into the parapet.
